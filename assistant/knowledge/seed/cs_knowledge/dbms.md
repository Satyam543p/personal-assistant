# Database Management Systems (DBMS)
- Relational Databases: Tables with schemas, ACID properties (Atomicity, Consistency, Isolation, Durability).
- Normalization: 1NF (atomic values), 2NF (no partial dependency), 3NF (no transitive dependency), BCNF.
- Indexing: B-Trees and B+ Trees for range queries and fast lookups, Hash indexes.
- Transactions & Concurrency: 2PL (Two-Phase Locking), Deadlock prevention and detection, Isolation levels (Read Uncommitted, Read Committed, Repeatable Read, Serializable).
